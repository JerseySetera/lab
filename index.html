04 / About

I’m Jersey.
Let’s make something strange.

I’m Jersey Zenith Setera, also known as zeints/Jerc — an Information Technology graduate and creative developer working across web development, graphic design, and digital media. I’m also the creator of Feint Lab and co-founder of NightPatch Dev.

That mix shaped how I work: I think about systems, but I’m equally interested in composition, typography, motion, visual identity, and how a person actually feels while using something — from websites and branding to Feint Lab clothing concepts and creative experiments.
