ABOUT — The person behind the pixels
04 / The person behind the pixels

I’m Jersey.
Design. Code. Culture.

I’m Jersey Zenith Setera, an Information Technology graduate and creative designer working across graphic design, web development, and digital media. I’m the founder and CEO of Feint Lab Collective and the Chief Operating Officer (COO) of NightPatch Dev.

My process combines systems thinking with a love for composition, typography, image-making, motion, and visual identity. I like work that feels considered but not too polished — a little raw, experimental, and unmistakably its own, whether it is a website, brand system, or clothing concept.
